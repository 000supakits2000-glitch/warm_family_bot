- บทพูด/เสียงพากย์: เสียงพากย์น้องมีนา: "หนูทำมันพังอีกแล้ว... ไม่ว่าทำอะไร หนูเสนอก็ดื้อและทำผิดพลาดไปหมดเลย..."
- Image/Video Prompt (EN): Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, medium shot of a lush Thai backyard garden during golden hour. In the background, a 68-year-old Thai grandfather with short white-grey hair, light brown cotton mandarin shirt, cream linen trousers holds a watering can. In the foreground, a sad 12-year-old Thai girl with straight black shoulder-length hair, pastel yellow clip, pale yellow t-shirt under light blue denim overalls sits on a wooden bench crying softly while holding a broken clay pot with a broken tiny plant. Soft golden sun rays, emotional atmosphere, cinematic video frame, vertical 9:16 aspect ratio
